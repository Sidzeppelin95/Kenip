Approach

A structured engineering lifecycle: understand, design, build, validate, and improve.

  1. 01 / UNDERSTAND

    Clarify the problem

    Agree objectives, stakeholders, constraints, risks, and what a useful outcome means.

  2. 02 / DESIGN

    Make trade-offs visible

    Compare feasible options, architecture, interfaces, security, and operational implications.

  3. 03 / BUILD

    Implement in focused increments

    Prefer small, reviewable changes with clear ownership and maintainability in mind.

  4. 04 / VALIDATE

    Test against the objective

    Use relevant tests, measurements, and acceptance criteria to check that the solution works as intended.

  5. 05 / IMPROVE

    Learn and iterate

    Document findings, address gaps, and identify the next highest-value improvement.
