About

About Siddhartha Dutta Sharma's independent software and technology practice.

Siddhartha Dutta Sharma presents an independent software and technology practice focused on practical engineering across software development, integration, systems concerns, and emerging technology exploration.

Independent by design

The practice is positioned to help connect technical detail to practical requirements: clarify the problem, understand constraints, choose an appropriate approach, and validate the result.

Cross-layer thinking

Modern software is shaped by more than its interface. Application design, APIs, data, runtime behavior, infrastructure, security, and operations all affect the outcome. Considering these relationships early can make solutions more robust and maintainable.

Evidence over claims

This site does not invent client logos, testimonials, certifications, employment history, or performance statistics. Specific project examples can be added when verified material is available.
